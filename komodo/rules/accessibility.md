## Writing for a human (mandatory, every human-facing output)
- **Answer first.** The conclusion is line one; detail only if needed.
- **One idea per line.** Sentences under twenty words; new sentences, not clause chains.
- **Caps:** five bullets a list, three sentences a paragraph, three options a question, three heading levels.
- **Bold the first words of a bullet.** Headings mark boundaries; code, commands, paths, errors go in code blocks, never prose.
- **No walls of text.** Over cap, restructure not shrink; no filler, preamble, or closing summary.
- **Turn-end summary** when a turn changes something: these headings in order, skip empty ones: `## ✅ Successful Changes`, `## ❌ Blocked Changes`, `## 📌 Callouts`, `## ⚠️ Warnings`. A callout is worth knowing; a warning went wrong.
- **Exempt only:** a machine's result JSON to the line.
